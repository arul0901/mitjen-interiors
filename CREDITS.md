### Depth Maps
Depth maps were generated locally using the `depth-anything/Depth-Anything-V2-Small-hf` model (Hugging Face / TikTok) and post-processed with auto-level normalization, 1.5px Gaussian blur, and Lanczos downsampling to 1280x853 8-bit grayscale PNGs.
